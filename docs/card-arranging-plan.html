Twenty-Eight · Feature plan

Let players arrange their cards

Players will be able to change the left-to-right order of the cards in their own hand. They can use a ready-made arrangement or place every card exactly where they want it.

This does not change the deal. The player keeps the same cards. Only their private on-screen order changes, so no server rule, score, or other player is affected.

What the player will see

The Arrange button stays hidden while the first four cards are used for bidding and trump selection. It appears near the player's hand only after the eighth card finishes its deal animation. From then on, it stays available until that hand ends—even during the player's own turn.

Suit groups

Keep each suit together. Inside a suit, use the game's strength order: J, 9, A, 10, K, Q, 8, 7.

Strongest first

Put all strong cards toward the left, regardless of suit.

Trump first

Put the known trump suit first, then group the other suits. This is disabled while trump is unknown.

Deal order

Return the cards to the order in which they were received.

Custom

Drag cards sideways and place them in any left-to-right order.

Exact player flow

  1. The first four cards arrive. The Arrange button is not shown yet.
  2. After trump selection, the final four cards arrive. The button appears when all eight cards have finished dealing.
  3. The player can tap Arrange at any time during the rest of the hand.
  4. Choosing a ready-made option rearranges the cards immediately and closes the menu.
  5. Choosing Custom starts arrangement mode and shows a clear Done button.
  6. The player drags a card sideways. Nearby cards slide apart to show where it will go.
  7. Releasing the card places it in the nearest slot. It cannot be accidentally played while arrangement mode is active.
  8. Tapping Done returns the hand to normal play controls without changing the chosen order.

Rules for important moments

Moment What happens
The final four cards arrive The button remains hidden during the animation. It appears only when the eighth card has landed. The cards remain in deal order until the player chooses an arrangement.
A card is played That card leaves the hand. Every remaining card keeps its relative position.
The player's turn begins during Custom mode Custom mode stays open, and the normal turn indicator remains visible. The player can finish arranging, tap Done, and then play a card.
Trump is unknown Trump first is disabled and explains that it becomes available when the player knows trump.
A new hand begins The previous order is cleared. The new cards stay in deal order, and Arrange remains hidden until all eight new cards have arrived.
The page reconnects If all eight cards had already been dealt, Arrange returns and the current order is rebuilt from the card IDs still in the hand.
The hand or match ends The arrangement menu closes and its button disappears.

How we will build it

  1. Add small, testable ordering rules for suit groups, strongest first, trump first, and deal order.
  2. Give the visual hand safe operations for moving one card or replacing the full visual order. The authoritative game hand will not be changed.
  3. Add the Arrange menu, preset buttons, Custom mode message, and Done button to the existing overlay.
  4. Separate card interaction into normal play mode and arrangement mode. In arrangement mode, a drag can only reorder cards.
  5. Remember the current hand's selected preset or custom order in local browser storage for reconnects. Clear it when a new hand starts. Nothing is sent to the server.
  6. Keep Arrange hidden during the first deal and final deal animation, then enable it after the eighth card lands. Connect it to card removal, new hands, reconnects, and match cleanup.
  7. Add unit and browser tests, then inspect desktop and phone screenshots to confirm that the fan and controls still fit.

Expected code areas

  • app/src/game/CardArrangement.ts — new pure card-ordering rules.
  • app/src/game/Hand.ts — reorder cards and animate them into fan slots.
  • app/src/interaction/DragControls.ts — separate play dragging from arrangement dragging.
  • app/src/game/GameState.ts — remember the mode and respond to game events.
  • app/src/ui/UiOverlay.ts and app/index.html — Arrange menu and responsive controls.
  • Frontend unit and Playwright tests — ordering, gestures, mobile layout, and play regressions.
No backend work is expected. Card identity and legal-play checks already use card IDs, so the server can continue treating the hand exactly as it does today.

Definition of done

  • Every preset produces the expected order.
  • The player can manually create any left-to-right order.
  • Arranging never sends a play command.
  • Playing a card still sends the correct card ID.
  • The remaining cards do not unexpectedly reorder after a play.
  • The Arrange button stays hidden until the eighth card finishes dealing.
  • After that point, arranging remains available throughout the hand, including during the player's turn.
  • The feature works with both mouse and touch.
  • The hand and buttons fit the 390 × 844 mobile layout.
  • Existing bidding, trump, legality, reconnect, and full-game tests still pass.

Not included

This feature will not exchange cards, deal new cards, randomize the deck, expose a player's order to opponents, or change any Twenty-Eight rule. It is a private visual preference only.
