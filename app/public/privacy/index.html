Privacy policy

Twenty-Eight, on the web at respawnfi.com and on Android. Effective 11 November 2026.

Twenty-Eight is a card game run by Corroded Labs ("we"). This policy covers the website at respawnfi.com and the Android app com.respawnfi.twentyeight. The short version: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We keep what the game needs to seat you at a table and keep score, and, from the Android app, diagnostics that help us fix problems on phones we don't have. You can turn those off.

What we collect

DataWhy
The display name you choose Shown to the other players at your table and, if you finish matches, on the leaderboard.
A random player ID, created on your device the first time you play Lets the game recognise you when you reconnect or rename yourself. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or any other identity.
Your game activity: the tables you join, your bids and cards, and the table reactions you send from the fixed set in the game To run the match. The game has no free-text chat.
Match results: when a match started and ended, the players in it, the winning team and the score For the leaderboard and each player's matches played and won.
Your IP address and basic connection details Needed to deliver the website and connect you to the game server. Our hosting providers may keep them briefly in their logs. The game server's own logs, also kept briefly, can include your display name. We don't use any of this to identify or profile you.
Diagnostics from the Android app: your phone's make, model and Android version, its graphics chip and driver, the screen's size and the sizes of the app's window and drawing, how smoothly the table draws, where your last few taps on the table landed and which card each hit, the app's version and its sound, speed and motion settings, and, on a phone that can't run the table, why not To find and fix problems on phones we don't have, such as a table that's slow or taps that miss. Each report gets its own random ID and carries no player ID or name, so it isn't linked to you. The app sends one when the table starts, after each hand and when you leave the app, at most once a minute. Turn them off under Diagnostics in the game's menu. The website sends none.

We don't collect your email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, advertising ID or device identifiers. The Android app asks only for internet access.

What stays on your device

Your display name, player ID, the match you're in (so you can rejoin it), and your sound, speed, motion and diagnostics settings are saved on your device: in the browser's local storage on the web, and in the app's private storage on Android. The app's data isn't included in Android backups. The website sets no cookies.

Who else handles data

We don't sell or rent data, and we don't share it for advertising. These services help run the game:

ServiceWhat it does
Fly.ioHosts the game server and its database, in Singapore.
NetlifyServes the website.
Google FontsServes the website's typeface. Your browser fetches it from Google, which sees your IP address.
Google PlayDistributes the Android app, under Google's own policy.

When you invite friends, the app opens your phone's share menu. Whatever you share goes to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp, under that app's policy.

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

A match in progress is saved so it survives server restarts, and is removed when the match ends. Match results, your name and your matches played and won are kept for the leaderboard until you ask us to delete them. Diagnostics reports are deleted after 30 days. Data on your device stays until you clear the site's data or uninstall the app.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can play under any name, and change it at any time. To have your match history and leaderboard entry deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, email mishraantash34@gmail.com with the display name you played under and roughly when. We'll reply within 30 days. To delete what's on your device, clear the site's data in your browser or uninstall the app.

Security

The website and the game connection are encrypted with TLS. No system is perfectly secure, but the game holds very little about you to begin with.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date at the top.
